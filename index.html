About

I am a first-year PhD student in the DataShape team at INRIA, Sophia-Antipolis. My research focuses on topological data analysis and computational geometry, exploring topological solutions to data-driven problems.

I completed my Master's in Data Science at Chennai Mathematical Institute, where I developed a strong foundation in probability theory before being drawn to the field of TDA.

Research Interests: Topological Data Analysis, Computational Geometry, Stochastic Processes
